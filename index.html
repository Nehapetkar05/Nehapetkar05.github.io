Aarogya AI: public health platform

React (Vite), Tailwind CSS, Node.js, Express, MySQL, Sequelize, Google Gemini

A full-stack health app where people can describe symptoms, check disease risk, and keep track of vaccinations, with an AI assistant that answers in several languages.

  • Google Gemini powers a multilingual health chatbot (English, Hindi, Marathi) with a built-in fallback, alongside a rule-based symptom checker that gives condition insights and care recommendations.
  • Five modular REST route groups cover authentication, health data, risk prediction, vaccination and user profiles.
  • Security built in: JWT-protected routes, bcrypt password hashing, input validation on login and registration, Helmet headers and CORS.
  • A rule-based risk module scores diabetes and hypertension risk from age, BMI, family history and lifestyle, then suggests prevention and screening steps.
  • A responsive interface with Chart.js dashboards, PDF report export, a vaccination scheduler, a first-aid guide and an emergency-contact directory.
